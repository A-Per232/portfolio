About me

Hey, I'm Arfaan.

I'm Arfaan, born and raised in Paramaribo, Suriname. For the first eleven years of my life, I mainly lived with my grandparents, where I learned many of the morals and values that continue to guide me today. From a young age, I enjoyed learning new things and constantly improving myself. I was fascinated by science fiction movies and animated films that depicted the future, and I spent countless hours imagining what technology might become. I always did well in school, but I never really found an outlet for my competitive side.

That changed when I discovered gaming. I became obsessed with improving my skills, competing with my friends, and constantly pushing myself to get better. Through a close friend, I was introduced to editing and coding. Because of my love for movies, I naturally gravitated toward editing and spent much of 2020 to 2023 developing those skills while spending more time in the digital world. More recently, coding has captured my attention. Watching people create everything from simple quality-of-life tools to robots entirely through code showed me how powerful software can be. The ability to build something from scratch that can automate tasks, solve problems, or help others sparked my interest and eventually led me to pursue Software Engineering.
